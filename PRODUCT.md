# PRODUCT.md: Freya Studio Cafe and Bakery

## Product Vision
Freya Studio Cafe and Bakery is an artisan micro-bakery and creative studio café located in Lipa City, Philippines. The landing page must reflect their core message: *"Crafted with intention. Served with soul."*

## Target Audience
- Coffee enthusiasts seeking specialty Vietnamese-inspired espresso creations (Salted Cream Coffee, Egg Coffee, Coconut Cold Brew).
- Pastry & Bakery lovers looking for freshly baked artisan breads and breakfast items.
- Creative workshop seekers interested in candle-making, art sessions, and community studio events.
- Local residents & travelers in Lipa City, Batangas.

## Key Features Required
1. **Hero Section**: High-impact visual storytelling with animated tagline, live status indicator ("Open Today in Lipa City"), primary CTAs (Explore Menu, Book Workshop).
2. **Artisan Menu Showcase**: Interactive filterable menu (Vietnamese Specialty Coffee, Micro-Bakery & Pastries, Signature Breakfast, Refreshing Elixirs) with detail modals, ingredients, and flavor notes.
3. **Vietnamese Coffee Spotlight**: Feature card dedicated to the Salted Cream Coffee & specialty brew creations with rich visual aesthetic.
4. **Creative Studio & Workshops**: Showcase upcoming workshops (e.g. "Candle-Making 102: Creative Candles") with interactive reservation modal.
5. **Atmosphere & Visual Gallery**: High-resolution gallery grid showing cafe interior, baked goods, coffee craft, and studio events.
6. **Customer Love / Reviews**: Testimonial carousel featuring 100% recommendation reviews from real visitors.
7. **Interactive Visit & Contact**: Operating hours, Google Map directions to JP Laurel Highway, Lipa City, direct call & Instagram integration.
8. **Interactive Coffee Customizer Widget**: Fun interactive element allowing users to "Build your FREYA Brew".

## Tech Stack
- Framework: Vite + React (JavaScript)
- Styling: Vanilla CSS with custom design system variables, glassmorphism, fluid responsive layouts.
- Typography: Google Fonts (Fraunces & Plus Jakarta Sans).
- Icons: Handcrafted inline SVGs.
